# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Kensaku to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming intent.
- Give and accept technical criticism gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and reproducible.
- Respect privacy: do not ask others to share file contents, paths, or personal
  data from their machines.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.
- Dismissing reports of problems without investigation.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
place where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate. Reports can be
sent privately to the maintainer listed on the repository profile. Reports will be
handled confidentially.
